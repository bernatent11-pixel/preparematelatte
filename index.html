What Is
Yerba Mate?

300 Years Of Daily Ritual

Yerba mate is the leaf of a South American holly tree, dried and steeped for centuries across Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay and southern Brazil. It has never really been a health trend there — it is simply how people start the day, and how they share one.

It isn't tea. It isn't coffee. It behaves like neither.

Mate carries caffeine for the lift, theobromine to widen and soften that lift, and naturally occurring antioxidants. Coffee gives you the first of those on its own — which is exactly why it spikes hard and drops just as hard. Mate climbs gently, holds for hours, and lets you back down without the crash.
